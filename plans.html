BEFORE YOU START

A few good questions.

Why does the Lite package cost ₹3,999?

It uses a simpler fixed-layout, one-page setup, limited sections and one revision. Custom visuals, enquiry forms and more pages require Starter, Professional or Premium.

Why are the Razorpay buttons more expensive than the Lite price?

Those buttons are advance amounts for the higher packages after a quotation is agreed. Lite Launch is a separate lower-scope offer, and payments are arranged individually.

Is the advance an extra charge?

No, it is included in the website total. ₹9,999 minus ₹5,000 leaves ₹4,999; ₹17,999 minus ₹9,000 leaves ₹8,999; and ₹29,999 minus ₹15,000 leaves ₹14,999.

Is the monthly plan required?

No. It is an optional service completely separate from website development.

Can I cancel maintenance?

Yes, subject to your written month-to-month agreement. Your domain and website remain under your control.

Does the website price include the domain?

No. Domains and third-party paid services are separate, preferably purchased in a client-owned account.

What counts as a small content update?

An approved text edit, picture swap, hours update, or minor link fix. New pages and features are not small edits.

Can I request a bigger website?

Yes. We discuss the features and pages you need and prepare a separate written estimate.
