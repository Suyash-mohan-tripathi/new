Privacy policy

What we collect

This site directs most enquiries to WhatsApp and offers optional Razorpay-hosted payment buttons for clients with agreed quotations. Pixelnest does not collect or store card or UPI credentials through its website. If you message us or make a payment, we may receive your name, contact details, business information, invoice details, payment status and other information you choose to share.

External services

WhatsApp, Razorpay and hosting services are operated by third parties and subject to their own terms and privacy policies. Razorpay processes checkout data; Pixelnest can access transaction information in its Razorpay account. The host may collect routine technical access logs.

Analytics and cookies

We have not intentionally installed advertising tracking pixels or custom analytics in this static site. If this changes, this disclosure should be updated.

Your questions

Contact Pixelnest via WhatsApp about information you have shared with us, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Website and service terms

Proposals and payments

Published package prices (₹3,999, ₹9,999, ₹17,999, ₹29,999) are indicative starting points. The ₹3,999 Lite Launch scope is separate from the Razorpay ₹5,000 / ₹9,000 / ₹15,000 advance buttons for larger approved projects. An advance is credited against the agreed total, not charged on top. Scope, costs, revisions, dates, ownership and payment stages must be agreed in writing before work begins. Razorpay buttons on our payment page are for individually approved deposits, not an automatic order or fixed public offer. We confirm completed payments in Razorpay before work starts. Any refunds or cancellations are handled according to the agreed written proposal and applicable law. Domains, paid integrations and third-party services may cost extra.

Project delivery and accounts

After scope approval and any agreed advance, we begin the project. Final handover follows the written agreement. Clients should control their own domain and hosting accounts where possible.

Third-party availability

External hosting, messaging and integration providers are not operated by Pixelnest. We cannot guarantee these services are available continuously.

Monthly website care

Monthly care covers only the services and response times agreed in writing. Major redesigns, new pages and additional features require separate estimates. Cancellation follows the active monthly terms.
