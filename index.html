EVERYTHING YOU SHOULD KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

What's the difference between ₹3,999 Lite and ₹9,999 Starter?

Lite Launch uses a preset page structure for your supplied content: one page, up to five sections, WhatsApp and map link, with one revision round. Starter offers a more customized one-page design, additional sections, a hosted enquiry form and two revision rounds. Domain, hosting and monthly care are separate for both.

Is the ₹5,000 Razorpay button for the ₹3,999 website?

No. The ₹5,000, ₹9,000 and ₹15,000 buttons are example advance amounts for separately agreed higher-tier projects. Never pay a button amount unless your written quotation matches it. For Lite Launch, ask Pixelnest for an individual payment arrangement.

Does the advance payment get added on top of the website price?

No. The advance is deducted from the agreed total. For example, on a ₹17,999 Professional site, a ₹9,000 advance leaves ₹8,999 to pay after approval, before final handover.

How much does it cost to build my website?

A basic one-page business website starts at ₹3,999. Larger websites or extra features need a custom quote.

How does the monthly website care plan work?

For ₹1,499 per month we perform up to two small content edits, check key links and forms, make a file backup and send a short work report. It's a separate, optional service.

Do I need to pay for my domain and hosting?

Domain names and any paid third-party services are billed separately. We can help you launch on suitable free hosting for a basic website.

How fast can my website go live?

A straightforward one-page site often takes 3–5 working days once all approved content is provided, depending on the agreed scope and review timeline.

Will the website belong to me?

Yes, subject to the written scope and licensing terms. We recommend keeping your domain and hosting accounts in your name and providing you the agreed website files.

Can I pause or cancel monthly care?

Monthly care is optional. You can stop renewals under your written service terms; stopping care does not mean losing your completed website.

What is included in a small update?

Short text edits, price or contact information changes, or swapping a supplied photo in an existing section. New pages, integrations and major redesigns are quoted separately.

Are the projects on your site all real clients?

Vibhog is a live family-business reference. The other projects are fictional design concepts, labeled clearly in the portfolio.
