GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked
questions.

How much does a website cost?

Our entry-level one-page business website starts at ₹3,999. Multi-page sites and advanced functionality need a separate quote.

Do I have to subscribe for maintenance?

No. Website creation and ongoing care are separate. Maintenance is entirely optional.

How long does a website take?

A simple one-page site may take about 3–5 working days after receiving approved content, depending on agreed scope and revisions.

Will I own my website?

Ownership and licensing are defined in the written agreement. We prefer the domain and hosting to be held in your own accounts.

Can you use my existing domain?

Usually, yes, if you own the domain and can provide the necessary access. External service fees are separate.

What can I request under monthly care?

Small approved text or image changes, routine checks, backups and basic link repairs. New pages and major redesigns are quoted separately.

What if I need to cancel monthly care?

You can stop the optional care plan as described in the monthly agreement. Your website does not disappear just because you cancel maintenance.

Are all showcased projects real clients?

No. Vibhog is presented as a real family-business showcase. Other website demos are original fictional concepts, clearly labeled on the Projects page.
